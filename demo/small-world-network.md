---
name: small-world-network
description: "Highly clustered, yet everything is close"
metadata:
  type: concept
---

A network with a high [[clustering-coefficient]] and short paths anyway. The [[watts-strogatz-model]] showed that rewiring a handful of edges at random is enough to produce it. See [[six-degrees-of-separation]].

See also: [[random-graph-model]], [[path]].
