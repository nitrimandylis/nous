---
name: preferential-attachment
description: "New nodes attach to nodes that already have edges"
metadata:
  type: concept
---

Growth where the chance of connecting to a [[node]] is proportional to its [[degree]]. Rich gets richer, and a [[scale-free-network]] falls out. [[barabasi]] and Albert wrote it down in 1999.

See also: [[hub-node]], [[power-law]].
