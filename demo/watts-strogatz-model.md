---
name: watts-strogatz-model
description: "A ring lattice with a few edges rewired"
metadata:
  type: concept
---

Start from a regular ring, rewire a small fraction of edges at random, and path lengths collapse while the [[clustering-coefficient]] barely moves. That is a [[small-world-network]].

See also: [[six-degrees-of-separation]], [[random-graph-model]].
